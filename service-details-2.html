Administrative & Warehouse Operational

I provide administrative and warehouse operational support, including data entry, inventory management, documentation, reporting, and coordination of daily activities. I help ensure data is accurate, workflows are organized, and warehouse operations run efficiently.

I manage, organize, validate, and process data to support administrative, operational, and reporting needs.

Focus on keeping data structured, accurate, and easy to understand, whether it is used for daily administration, inventory records, reporting, or simple data analysis.

  • Data Entry & Data Organization
  • Excel Formulas & Data Analysis
  • Reporting & Data Validation

I work with Microsoft Excel and Google Sheets to organize datasets, create structured tables, clean and validate information, and prepare data for reporting. My Excel skills include Pivot Tables, VLOOKUP/XLOOKUP, Data Validation, Conditional Formatting, and other functions that can help simplify repetitive tasks and improve data accuracy. I can also assist with preparing reports and summaries from raw data, making information easier to review and use for day-to-day decision-making. My approach is focused on accuracy, consistency, and practical organization, while keeping spreadsheets easy to maintain and understand.

Through my experience with administrative and operational data, I continue to improve my ability to work with spreadsheets and develop efficient ways to manage information.
